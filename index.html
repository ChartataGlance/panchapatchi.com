How moon block is calculated

Moon block is based on Sun-Moon angular separation.

phaseAngle = ((moonLongitude - sunLongitude) + 360) % 360 moonBlock = Math.floor(phaseAngle / 12)

30 blocks × 12° = 360°. Block 0 is New Moon at 3 o'clock. Block 15 is Full Moon at 9 o'clock.

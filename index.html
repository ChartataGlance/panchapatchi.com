How moon position is calculated

Moon age is estimated from a known new moon date. Synodic month length is about 29.530588853 days.

Phase angle = moon age ÷ 29.530588853 × 360.

Section number = floor(phase angle ÷ 12). So 0° to 180° gives 15 rising sections, and 180° to 360° gives 15 falling sections.
